Privacy
Policy

Your spring journal belongs to you. This page explains what Soakfolio keeps on your device and when an outside service receives information.

01 / What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Soakfolio iOS app. Soakfolio does not require an account to create a journal, route card, stamp impression, or PDF. It does not request your device's live location. Links you choose to visit in a browser are governed by the linked site's own privacy practices.

02 / Content kept on your device

The app saves your journal text, selected or captured photos, route cards, stamp impressions, generated PDFs, and related dates and settings in local app storage. It also caches catalog records, map resources, and images so previously loaded material can remain available. Purchase delivery records and your Seal balance are kept on the device to prevent duplicate delivery or charges.

Soakfolio does not upload your journal text, private photos, voice notes, or exported PDFs to its catalog service. If you use the iOS sharing sheet, the destination you choose receives the file or content you share under that destination's terms.

03 / Photos, camera, microphone, and notifications

When you choose a photo, the app asks for photo-library permission and uses the selected image for your journal. The system photo picker can still let you choose individual photos if full library access is off. When you take a photo, the app asks for camera permission and keeps the captured image with your journal. When you use voice input, microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested so spoken words can become editable text. The recording is passed to Apple's speech-recognition service for transcription, subject to Apple's privacy practices. The app removes its temporary recording after that action and does not publish it.

When you first reach the home screen, iOS may ask whether Soakfolio can send notifications. You can allow or decline. You can change any permission later in iOS Settings. Permission choices are controlled by iOS; declining a permission may limit the associated feature.

04 / Online catalog, maps, and third-party services

Browsing the spring catalog, downloading place photographs, loading map tiles, and searching mapped places can make network requests to Soakfolio's catalog provider and third-party map, search, or image providers, including OpenStreetMap-related services and OpenFreeMap. A search request can include the place name you enter. These services can receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and request details. The app does not send them your private journal, photo library, or audio.

App Store purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes purchase and payment information under its own privacy policy; Soakfolio receives transaction details needed to deliver Seals. We do not receive your full payment card details.

05 / Seals and purchase records

Seals are optional consumable in-app purchases. The app stores the delivered transaction identifier and resulting balance locally to avoid duplicate delivery and to account for completed uses. StoreKit may provide transaction updates for purchases made with your Apple Account. Apple controls payment, billing, and purchase history.

06 / Control and deletion

You can review and delete journals, route cards, and saved PDFs in the app. Settings also provides Clear All Data for local journal material, route cards, imported photos, PDFs, and caches. Purchased Seal balances and transaction records are retained by that action to protect purchase accounting. Removing the app removes its local data from that device, subject to any iOS backup you control. Copies you shared elsewhere must be managed with the receiving service.

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current text.
